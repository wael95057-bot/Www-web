المعادلات المستخدمة

CAPEX = Area × 2500 Annual Generation = Solar MWp × 1000 × 1800 OPEX = CAPEX × 0.02 Payback = CAPEX ÷ (Annual Savings − OPEX) NPV = Σ [Cashflow / (1+r)^t] − CAPEX Availability = ((Operating − Downtime) / Operating) × 100 MTBF = (Operating − Downtime) / Failures MTTR = Downtime / Failures CO₂ Offset = Energy × 0.708 + Trees × 0.022 Grid Offset % = min(Solar + BESS Discharge, Load) ÷ Load × 100 Grid Dependency % = Grid Import ÷ Load × 100 BESS Remaining Energy = Capacity × SOC% BESS Autonomy (h) = Remaining Energy ÷ Discharge Rate Dead Load (kg) = Area × Σ(Component Weight/m²) × (1 + Safety Margin%), Margin ≥ 15% Wind Velocity Pressure q (Pa) = 0.613 × V² × Height Factor [simplified, ASCE 7-style] Uplift Force (kgf) = q × Cp(tilt) × Area ÷ 9.81 BESS Load/m² = BESS Weight ÷ Effective Footprint Area (Spreader Beams: ×1.4) Slab Utilization % = (BESS Load/m² ÷ Allowable Slab Capacity) × 100
